# Content Sources & Research Provenance

This document records the official government, tourism board, and temple administration sources referenced to write factual, practical route and shrine descriptions across all 13 Char Dham day-detail pages.

---

### Primary Official Sources

1. **Uttarakhand Tourism Development Board (UTDB)**
   - **Website:** [https://uttarakhandtourism.gov.in](https://uttarakhandtourism.gov.in)
   - **Used For:** Descriptions of Haridwar, Yamunotri route (Janki Chatti, Surya Kund, Divya Shila), Gangotri route (Gangnani Kund, Harsil Valley, Bhagirath Shila), Kedarnath trek route (Gaurikund, Mandakini river path), Badrinath (Mana Village, Vyas Gufa, Bhim Pul, Charan Paduka), Tungnath / Chopta, and Rishikesh.

2. **Shri Badrinath - Shri Kedarnath Temple Committee (BKTC)**
   - **Website:** [https://badrinath-kedarnath.gov.in](https://badrinath-kedarnath.gov.in)
   - **Used For:** Sacred temple traditions, historical Jyotirlinga background of Kedarnath Dham, Badri Vishal temple background, and worship timings overview.

3. **Official District Portals (National Informatics Centre, Uttarakhand)**
   - **District Haridwar:** [https://haridwar.nic.in](https://haridwar.nic.in) (Haridwar arrival and Ganga ghats)
   - **District Uttarkashi:** [https://uttarkashi.nic.in](https://uttarkashi.nic.in) (Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Gupteshwar Mahadev, Lakhamandal, Gangotri, Yamunotri)
   - **District Rudraprayag:** [https://rudraprayag.nic.in](https://rudraprayag.nic.in) (Sonprayag, Triyuginarayan Temple, Kedarnath Dham)
   - **District Chamoli:** [https://chamoli.nic.in](https://chamoli.nic.in) (Badrinath Dham, Mana border village, Tungnath, Pipalkoti)
