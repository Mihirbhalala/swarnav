## Images That Could Not Be Legally Obtained (Checklist for Business Owner)

Where verified photos of specific places were not available with open commercial-reuse licenses, clean text-only cards are displayed instead of unrelated placeholders.

1. **Neelkanth Mahadev Temple** (Rishikesh/Pauri) — Verified temple facade or entrance photo.

3. **Kashi Vishwanath Temple** (Uttarkashi) — Temple courtyard and Shakti Trishul.

5. **Triyuginarayan Temple** (Rudraprayag) — Ancient Akhand Dhuni temple.
